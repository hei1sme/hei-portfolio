## The challenge

BioListen VN explores how ecological acoustics can support biodiversity monitoring. Model outputs need to become something a ranger can inspect: an audio input, a visible spectrogram, an alert and an understandable report.

We built the prototype as **Team NeuraX.ai**, a three-person FPT University team, for **Vietnam AI Innovation Challenge 2026**, held on 17–19 July at FPT Tower in Hanoi.

## My contribution

I worked on the UI and LLM layer. The [task board for my work](https://github.com/hei1sme/BioListen-VN/blob/master/docs/tasks/TASKS_HUNG.md) records:

- Audio-upload and microphone-input views.
- Spectrogram, alert and monitoring interfaces.
- Integration of backend API responses into the UI.
- Vietnamese prompts that turn species and threat information into ranger reports.

The team also worked on the acoustic model and backend. I distinguish that wider architecture from my own UI and LLM contribution.

## Competition result

Our team placed **6th in Round 1** and was eliminated in a later round.
