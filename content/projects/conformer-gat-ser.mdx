## Motivation

Emotion recognition systems often operate as black boxes, especially when they merge acoustic signals with transcripts.  
This project explores how **Wav2Vec 2.0**, **RoBERTa**, and **Graph Attention Networks (GATv2)** can cooperate to provide both accuracy and interpretability.

## Architecture

- **Wav2Vec 2.0** encodes rich audio representations capturing prosody, spectral features, and temporal cues
- **RoBERTa** produces contextual embeddings for transcripts
- **GATv2 fusion layer** models cross-modal dependencies with dynamic conversational graphs modeling temporal and speaker interactions
- A shared decision head predicts categorical emotions with **flexible 4/5/6-class emotion heads**

## Explainability Toolkit

- Implementing **intrinsic XAI** using attention weights for decision interpretability
- Built a reusable pipeline featuring SHAP value aggregation, Integrated Gradients, and counterfactual analysis
- Explanations surface *which* words or time segments contribute to a prediction and *how* the modalities corroborate each other

## Current Progress

- Benchmarking on **IEMOCAP**, **RAVDESS**, and **MELD** datasets
- Designing dynamic conversational graphs modeling temporal and speaker interactions
- Validating that GATv2-based fusion improves macro-F1 compared to late-concatenation baselines
- Stress-testing interpretability outputs with qualitative reviews from peers
