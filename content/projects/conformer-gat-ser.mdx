## Motivation

Emotion recognition systems often operate as black boxes, especially when they merge acoustic signals with transcripts.  
This project explores how **Conformer encoders** and **Graph Attention Networks (GATs)** can cooperate to provide both accuracy and interpretability.

## Architecture Sketch

- **Conformer branch** encodes prosody, spectral features, and temporal cues from audio.
- **Text branch** produces contextual embeddings for transcripts and forced alignments.
- **GAT fusion layer** models cross-modal dependencies, letting important syllables attend to emotive acoustic frames.
- A shared decision head predicts categorical emotions while exposing attention weights for inspection.

## Explainability Toolkit

- Built a reusable pipeline featuring SHAP value aggregation, Integrated Gradients, and counterfactual analysis (DiCE-style).
- Explanations surface *which* words or time segments contribute to a prediction and *how* the modalities corroborate each other.

## Current Progress

- Standing up reproducible experiments on the **IEMOCAP** and **RAVDESS** datasets.
- Validating that GAT-based fusion improves macro-F1 compared to late-concatenation baselines.
- Stress-testing interpretability outputs with qualitative reviews from peers.

## Next Steps
