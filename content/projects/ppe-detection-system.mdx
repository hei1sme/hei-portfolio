## Problem Statement

Construction supervisors needed a lightweight way to verify whether workers were wearing mandatory protective equipment.  
Manual inspection was inconsistent, and existing tools struggled with multiple classes simultaneously.

## Solution

- Trained **YOLOv8** models to detect nine PPE categories (helmet, vest, gloves, boots, and their absence states).
- Added a compliance engine that labels frames as **SAFE** or **UNSAFE** based on detected combinations.
- Packaged the pipeline inside a **Streamlit** app capable of processing webcam feeds, uploaded videos, or still images.

## Features

- Real-time inference with configurable confidence thresholds and per-class alerting.
- Exportable compliance logs for auditing and incident follow-up.
- Hardware-friendly deployment footprint suitable for field laptops.

## Result

The project became the capstone deliverable for **DPL302m** and a foundation for future industrial safety experiments at SpeedyLabX.
